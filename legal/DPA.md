This DPA forms part of the agreement ("Agreement") between **[CUSTOMER LEGAL NAME]** ("Customer") and **[LEGAL ENTITY NAME, trading as Novus Forge]**, [ADDRESS], India ("Provider"), for the Triage Desk service. It applies where Provider processes Personal Data on Customer's behalf. Terms such as "controller", "processor", "Personal Data" and "Personal Data Breach" have the meaning in applicable Data Protection Law ([GDPR / UK GDPR / India DPDP Act 2023 and rules / other — lawyer to confirm]).

## 1. Roles
Customer is controller (data fiduciary); Provider is processor (data processor) for Customer Data. Provider is controller for account administration, billing, security and service-improvement data about Customer's users (see Privacy Policy).

## 2. Instructions
Provider processes Customer Data only to provide the Service and per Customer's documented instructions (the Agreement, this DPA, and Customer's use of the Service's features). Provider will tell Customer if it believes an instruction breaches Data Protection Law.

## 3. Details of processing (Annex A)
- **Subject matter / duration:** providing the Service for the term of the Agreement plus the deletion period in §9.
- **Nature and purpose:** hosting, storing and displaying tickets, KB, known issues and corrections; authenticating users; AI-assisted triage on user request; backups; billing and transactional email.
- **Data subjects:** Customer's users; Customer's customers and other individuals mentioned in tickets.
- **Personal Data types:** user name, email, role, password hash; ticket content and attachments (screenshots) as entered by Customer, which may include contact details, names and free-text information; audit and usage records.
- **Special categories:** the Service is not designed for special-category or children's data. Customer will not submit it unless agreed in writing. [Lawyer to confirm position.]

## 4. Customer responsibilities
Customer has a lawful basis for the data it submits, gives required notices, and decides what is sent to AI analysis. Customer acknowledges that server-side redaction is pattern-based (emails, phone, card/ID numbers, keys, passwords, IPs) and does not mask names, addresses, free text or screenshot content.

## 5. Confidentiality
Provider ensures personnel with access are bound by confidentiality and access Customer Data only as needed.

## 6. Security (Annex B)
Provider maintains measures appropriate to the risk, including: scrypt password hashing; HTTPS-only HttpOnly session cookies; per-tenant filtering on every data query; role-based access (admin/agent/viewer); append-only audit log; login, sign-up and password-reset throttling; hashed single-use email tokens; backup files with owner-only permissions; origin checks on state-changing requests. ## 9. Return and deletion
On termination, Customer may export its data for [30] days. After that Provider deletes Customer Data from live systems within [30] days, except where law requires retention. ## 10. Data subject requests
Provider will promptly forward requests it receives about Customer Data and, taking into account the nature of processing, reasonably help Customer respond. Customer can edit and delete most Customer Data itself in the Service.

Provider notifies Customer without undue delay and within [48/72] hours of becoming aware of a Personal Data Breach affecting Customer Data, with the information then available, and cooperates in investigation and mitigation.
