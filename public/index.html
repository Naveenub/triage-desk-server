Audit log

Last 100 actions by everyone on this page: who added, assigned, changed, resolved or deleted tickets, what was sent to Claude, and exports. Ticket text is never logged. Anyone with edit access could delete entries, so this is a record for the team, not tamper-proof evidence.

Knowledge base

Add help articles. Suggested replies quote or cite them, and Claude is told to take product facts from them and nothing else. Shared with everyone who can edit this page. Matching is by keyword, so articles match best in the language the customer writes in.

Known issues

Keep a short list of issues your team already knows about. When a new ticket contains your keywords, the Known issue line says Yes and links to the issue. This list is typed in by your team. The desk does not read your bug tracker, so update it when issues open or are fixed. Shared with everyone who can edit this page.

Accuracy check

Measure how often the keyword rules and Claude agree with your own labels. Upload a CSV of up to 100 tickets with the columns text, category and priority. Categories: Bug, How-to, Feature request, Billing, Account, Integration, Security, Data, Performance. Priority: P1 to P4. Claude's check sends the ticket text to Claude, so use tickets you are comfortable sharing. When the hide box is ticked, emails, phones, card and ID numbers, keys, passwords, IPs and link tokens are masked first; names, addresses and free-text details are not.
